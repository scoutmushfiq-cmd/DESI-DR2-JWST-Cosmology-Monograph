# Figure Provenance

Per-figure provenance for every `\includegraphics` target in
`cosmology_monograph_full.tex`, as referenced by the Reproducibility &
Code Availability Statement. Generator status categories:

- **released** — regenerated by a script shipped in `verification/` from data/chains shipped in this repository.
- **book-pipeline** — produced by the author's separate book-figure pipeline; the underlying numbers are quoted (with references) in the corresponding manuscript section or caption, and the pipeline scripts accompany the final journal submission package.
- **schematic** — illustrative artwork (no data content).
