# Hostile Referee Report — Round 15

**Scope:** audit of the Round-14 fix pass itself (the two caption re-homes, the fig13 gridline redraw, the orphan-artifact/template cleanup, and the m1/m2 disclosures), plus a full-artifact sweep of everything the Round-14 edits touched.

**Verdict: MAJOR REVISION.** The Round-14 pass fixed its targets but introduced a new misattribution in the tension-matrix caption, exposed a pre-existing misattribution in the compressed-CMB validation paragraph, and asserted an uncertainty scale ("± O(0.4) nats") that is derived nowhere.

## Major findings

### M1 — the Round-14 tension-matrix caption misattributes three published rows to our chains

The Round-14 rewrite of the `tab:tension_matrix` caption states that "for the rows that are fits of this work (the DESI DR2 + CMB rows and the Sound-Horizon-Free row), [the values come] from our committed MCMC chains (the compressed- and full-likelihood values agree exactly; Part VII-B)."

This contradicts the table itself:

- `DESI DR2 + CMB ΛCDM` row: **68.17 ± 0.28** — the published DESI DR2 value. Our committed chain `chain_lcdm_desi_cmb.npy` (96,000 draws) gives **H0 = 68.35 ± 0.33** (Ωm 0.3007 ± 0.0049), which the monograph itself quotes at the seed-stability paragraph and the Om(z) section.
- `DESI DR2 + BBN ΛCDM` row: **68.51 ± 0.58** — published transcription; no dedicated chain of ours is quoted for it.
- `DESI DR2 + CMB + PP w0wa` row: **67.51 ± 0.59** — published transcription; our w0wa chain gives 63.60 ± 1.91 for DESI+CMB (without PP), a different configuration.

Only the Sound-Horizon-Free row (68.19 ± 1.45) is a fit of this work. The imported clause "the compressed- and full-likelihood values agree exactly" belongs to the Part VII-B compressed-CMB validation paragraph (where it describes DESI's own published compressed-based constraints against DESI's full-CamSpec chains), not to this table. A referee who checks one row against the chains finds the caption wrong.

### M2 — the Part VII-B compressed-CMB validation paragraph misattributes DESI's published values to "the compressed-chain posteriors"

The paragraph states: "for flat ΛCDM + DESI DR2 BAO the compressed and full-likelihood constraints agree exactly (H0 = 68.17 ± 0.28 vs. the official 68.17 ± 0.28; Ωm = 0.3027 ± 0.0036 vs. 0.3027 ± 0.0036), and for w0waCDM the compressed-chain posteriors (w0 = −0.43 ± 0.22, wa = −1.72 ± 0.64, H0 = 63.7+1.7−2.2) reproduce the full-Planck DESI DR2 values (−0.42 ± 0.21, −1.75 ± 0.58, 63.6+1.4−2.1) to < 0.1σ in every parameter."

Every number in the first set is DESI's published value, not ours. Our committed chains give ΛCDM H0 = 68.35 ± 0.33 / Ωm = 0.3007 ± 0.0049 and w0wa (w0, wa, H0) = (−0.48 ± 0.31, −1.71 ± 0.72, 63.60 ± 1.91). As written, the paragraph claims an exact reproduction that our chains do not deliver (H0 differs by 0.4σ) — and it never states the actual, perfectly good agreement of our chains with the official values (≤ 0.4σ in every parameter). The true validation statement is stronger than a false exactness claim; the paragraph must say both, with correct attribution.

### M3 — the "± O(0.4) nats" Savage–Dickey systematic scale is derived nowhere, and the canonical artifact has no generator

The Round-14 m2 disclosure asserts the canonical CPL Savage–Dickey value "should be read as ± O(0.4) nats." No artifact or script derives this scale; the only logged evidence is a single two-chain subset shifted by +0.76. Worse, `savage_dickey_canonical.json` — now load-bearing in the semantics paragraph — is generated by no committed script (only its `V15_scott_2chains` field is reproducible, via the `run_mirage_sd.py` cross-check, which reproduces it to full precision: 3.3552412218201786).

Direct recomputation from the committed chains (this audit, to be released as `run_sd_canonical.py`):

- **Every lnB row of the canonical artifact reproduces exactly** from the committed chains with the same `cosmo.savage_dickey` estimator: wCDM (1-D w0, V = 2.5, chains s11+s22) → −1.4939; CPL (2-D (w0, wa), V = 15, all four DESI+CMB chains, n = 403,200) → **2.6005**; V = 13.5 → 2.7059; two-chain subset (s111+s222) → 3.3552; JBP → −0.8284; Log f_DE → 1.8232.
- **Derived scatter (replaces the invented ± O(0.4)):** the four single-chain values are 1.78, 3.90, 2.74, 2.31 (std **0.90**); the six two-chain subsets span 2.08–3.36 (std **0.51**, mean 2.54); the all-chain value is 2.60. The +0.76 shift of the s111+s222 subset is unremarkable within this scatter.

## Minor findings

### m1 — boundary mismatch between the two severity statements the Round-14 edits wrote side by side

The fig8 caption says cells "at or above the 3σ severe-tension threshold" are labeled SEVERE (matching the generator's `sev()` rule, ≥ 3σ), while the table caption says cells "exceeding 3σ" are set in bold (strictly >). No cell equals exactly 3σ so no cell changes status, but the two adjacent sentences state different rules. Align both on "at or above."

### m2 — the z_c block of `savage_dickey_canonical.json` is not reproducible by any committed script

The `zc` medians (0.495, 0.345) match the tex and were verified in Round 14, but no committed script generates them; a crossing-subset recomputation from the four committed CPL chains gives the median to 4 × 10⁻⁶ but std 0.123 vs. the artifact's 0.077 and frac_crossing 0.9750 vs. 0.9749. The block predates the committed scripts. Not fixed silently: the new generator reproduces and asserts the lnB rows and documents that the z_c block is retained from the artifact as a legacy computation.
